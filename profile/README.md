## Beyond Media. Towards Meaningful Change.

Founded in 2012 and headquartered in Paço de Arcos, Portugal, TELENIMA is an audiovisual media company whose work has evolved beyond conventional production towards a broader commitment to awareness, prevention and meaningful social impact.

We believe that audiovisual communication is not merely a means of telling stories. It can reveal difficult realities, challenge indifference and bring people face to face with truths they might otherwise choose to ignore.

## Confronting Reality. Awakening Awareness.

Some realities are uncomfortable. Some are easier to overlook than to confront. Yet the issues that threaten human dignity, safety and well-being cannot always be addressed through reassuring words alone.

TELENIMA has embraced a direct and uncompromising approach to awareness-raising, using the power of visual communication to draw attention to critical issues and encourage reflection, prevention, understanding and action.

## Our Priority Areas

TELENIMA's work increasingly centre on initiatives that contribute to a more informed, safer and more responsible society.

Our priority areas include:

- **Public health and prevention** - raising awareness of health risks and supporting prevention campaigns.
- **Cybersecurity and digital safety** - promoting awareness of online threats and responsible digital practices.
- **Child protection and human dignity** - drawing attention to the vulnerability of children, particularly in hostile and dangerous environments.
- **Environment and collective responsibility** - encouraging greater awareness of environmental challenges.
- **Solidarity and public awareness** - supporting causes such as blood donation and campaigns addressing serious illnesses, conflict and other critical situations.

These concerns reflect a conviction that communication can serve a purpose greater than visibility alone.

## Creativity with Intention

TELENIMA's audiovisual background remains part of its identity. Creative and production work may take different forms, but the guiding question is not simply what can be produced. It is what that work can communicate, reveal or contribute.

We value collaboration with other entities and initiatives when it allows creative work to serve a meaningful purpose.

## Our Digital Dimension

Launched in 2017, eTELENIMA represents TELENIMA's digital dimension, with a presence in Vitry-sur-Seine, France. Together, the two names reflect an identity shaped by audiovisual communication, digital creativity and an international outlook.

## Our Guiding Principle

We do not believe that every truth is comfortable, or that every important message should be easy to receive.

We believe that some realities must be brought into the light - not to exploit suffering, but to confront indifference; not to provoke for the sake of provocation, but to awaken awareness and encourage change.

Because seeing is not always the same as understanding. And understanding can be the beginning of action.
